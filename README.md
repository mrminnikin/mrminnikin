# Steven Minnikin

**Principal AI Architect · Agentic AI in regulated and safety-critical industries**
Founder of Minibit Ltd · Newcastle upon Tyne, UK · [minnikin.ai](https://minnikin.ai)

I design and build agentic AI systems for industries where mistakes are regulated: MCP servers, Claude connectors, agents with a person in the loop, and the guardrails around them. Before AI, I spent 25 years in software, from Rolls-Royce's nuclear, aerospace and power work to seven years running technology at an FCA-regulated financial advisory firm with over £1bn under management.

### What I build

- **Agents with human approval**: nothing is saved or sent until a person signs it off
- **AI safety in practice**: evals, guardrails, prompt-injection defence, spending caps and audit trails
- **Integrations for UK advice firms**, including Intelliflo Office
